Frequently Asked Questions

What does ODIYA do?

ODIYA helps you find and share your exact GPS location instantly, even when you're offline. It identifies nearby landmarks and provides your coordinates so you can communicate your position during emergencies, travel, or outdoor activities.

Does ODIYA contact emergency services directly?

No. ODIYA does not send distress signals or contact emergency services (such as 911) directly. It is a location-sharing tool that helps you determine and communicate your position. To contact emergency services, please call your local emergency number directly.

Does ODIYA work offline?

Yes. ODIYA can display your GPS coordinates even without an internet connection. However, some features like landmark identification and map display require an internet connection.

How do I share my location?

Tap the share button within the app to send your current GPS coordinates and nearby landmark information via text message, email, or any other sharing method available on your device.

Report an Issue

If you encounter any bugs, crashes, or other issues while using ODIYA, please email us at hello@odiya.info with the following information:

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • A description of the issue
  • Steps to reproduce the problem

Important: ODIYA is not a substitute for emergency services. In case of an emergency, always call your local emergency number (e.g., 911 in the US, 119 in Korea) directly. ODIYA provides location information only and does not dispatch emergency responders.
